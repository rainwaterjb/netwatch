        Device recognition

        NetWatch works out what each device is (phone, TV, printer…) from what it already announces on your network: DHCP, Bonjour/mDNS and UPnP. It never logs in to anything.

        Tags

        Add tags to devices from their detail page (click a device's name). Then turn alerts on or off for a whole group here.

            Your account

            Accounts

            Give each person their own sign-in. Admins can change everything; viewers can look but not change anything. The built-in admin from .env always works, so you can't lock yourself out.
